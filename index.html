About Berean Baptist Academy

Cultivating Character,
Inspiring Lifelong Excellence

Nestled in the green hills of Khumji, Noney District, Berean Baptist Academy was established to provide sound academic training infused with Biblical ethics. We cater specifically to youth progressing through their most vital educational milestones—from Class 6th to Class 12th.

The presence of the river by our campus fosters peace, contemplative study, and close connection with nature, giving our students the ideal foundation to grow intellectually, spiritually, and socially.

Christian Principles

Integrity, service, and high moral standards.

Academic Focus

STEM laboratories & Arts education.
