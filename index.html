Who We Are

Rooted in Faith,
Engineered for Academic Brilliance

Berean Baptist Academy, established in Khumji, Noney District, is devoted to providing quality, holistic, and value-based education. Inspired by Biblical wisdom and modern educational paradigms, we instill discipline, leadership, and moral rectitude in every student.

Our position beside the river not only provides picturesque vistas of Manipur's hill landscapes, but also ensures a peaceful atmosphere free from urban distractions—the perfect sanctuary for adolescent development.
